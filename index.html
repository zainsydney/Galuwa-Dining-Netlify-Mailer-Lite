Opening Spring 2026

Opening Spring 2026, Galuwa Dining is a new dining and events destination nestled within the beautiful Lane Cove Golf Course, just 15 minutes from Sydney's CBD.

Designed to bring people together over exceptional food, warm hospitality and beautiful surroundings, Galuwa Dining will offer a welcoming space to dine, celebrate and connect. From relaxed breakfasts and long lunches to evening drinks, intimate dinners and memorable celebrations, every experience has been thoughtfully considered.

Alongside the restaurant, Galuwa Dining will feature a collection of contemporary event spaces overlooking the golf course, ideal for corporate meetings, conferences, weddings, celebrations and community events. Flexible spaces, tailored catering and personalised service can host up to 500 guests when combined.

Bookings are now open for end-of-year functions and events. Restaurant menus will be released closer to opening; sign up for news, menu announcements and exclusive invitations as we count down to Spring 2026.
